# Email Marketing Studio

This studio is for planning and writing email campaigns for a small business.

## Folders

- `brand/` holds the business's voice, style, and key facts that every email should follow.
- `leads/` holds lists and notes about the people and groups the emails are aimed at.
- `campaigns/` holds campaign plans and the emails written for each one.
- `notes/` holds research, ideas, and decisions about how the studio works.
