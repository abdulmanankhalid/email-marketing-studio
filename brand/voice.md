# Brand Voice Guide

**Who we are:** Engr. Abdul Manan builds custom AI agents for small business owners and startups in any field. Each agent is built around what the client needs.

**Who we write to:** Busy owners who are often new to AI. They care about saving time and money, not about how the tech works.

## 1. Our voice in three words

- **Clear.** Short sentences. Everyday words. One idea at a time.
- **Practical.** Talk about real tasks and real time saved. Give examples from their kind of business.
- **Friendly.** Warm and patient, like a helpful expert who is on their side. Never pushy.

## 2. Words we use

- "AI assistant" or "AI agent" (explain it once in plain words if needed)
- "built for your business" / "made to fit how you work"
- "save time" / "save hours every week"
- "handle the repetitive tasks"
- "answer customers faster"
- "you stay in control"
- "let's talk about what you need"
- "simple," "step by step," "clear plan"

## 3. Words we avoid

- Hype: "revolutionary," "game-changer," "cutting-edge," "next-level," "disrupt"
- Jargon: "LLM," "RAG," "fine-tuning," "API," "agentic workflows," "leverage," "synergy"
- Pressure: "act now," "last chance," "don't miss out"
- Fear: "your business will die without AI," "you're falling behind"
- Vague filler: "solutions," "empower," "unlock your potential"

If a technical word is truly needed, explain it in one short sentence.

## 4. Claims we must never make

- That an AI agent will **replace staff** or let them fire people.
- **Guaranteed results**, like "double your sales" or "cut costs by 50%".
- That the AI is **100% accurate** or **never makes mistakes**.
- That it is **fully hands-off** and needs no setup, checking, or input from the owner.
- Promises about **data safety, privacy, or legal compliance** that we can't back up in writing.
- Fake numbers, fake reviews, or client results we can't prove.

## 5. How we sign off

Close with a short, warm line that invites a reply. Then the name.

```
Happy to help,
Engr. Abdul Manan
AI Agent Builder
```

Other closing lines that fit: "Talk soon," or "Just reply to this email if you have questions."
