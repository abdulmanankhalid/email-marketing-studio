# Campaign Plan: AI Agent Service Launch

**Status:** Draft plan. No emails have been sent.
**Dates:** Monday 7 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027 (4 weeks)
**Channel:** Email only. No paid ads.

---

## 1. Campaign Overview

**Campaign name:** "Built for Your Business" Launch

**Summary:** Tell our waitlist that the AI agent service is open, and invite them to book a free setup call.

**Main goal:** 30 waitlist members book a free setup call by 3 January 2027. That is about 6% of 500 people.

**Other goals:**
- Learn which of the three groups is most interested, so we know who to focus on next.
- Collect the most common questions and worries, to use in future emails and on the website.
- Hold at least 20 of the 30 calls (some people will book and not show up).

---

## 2. Target Audience

All 500 people chose to join the waitlist, so they already know us a little. This is a warm list, not a cold one.

| Group | Who they are | What they want | What worries them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Small business owners** | Owners of local or service businesses (clinics, salons, trades, consultants) | Answer customers faster, stop doing the same admin every day | Cost, "is this too technical for me?", losing the personal touch |
| **Online store owners** | People who run an online shop | Handle common customer questions (order status, returns, sizing) and save hours every week | Wrong answers to customers, setup time, cost per month |
| **Agencies** | Small marketing, design, or service agencies | Handle repetitive tasks like client updates, reports, and new enquiries | Quality of work, fitting into how their team already works |

**Buying stage:** Most are in the "interested but not sure yet" stage. They joined a waitlist, so they are curious. The free call is the right next step. It is low effort for them and does not ask them to buy anything.

**Gaps to fill before launch:**
- We don't know how many people are in each group. Tag the list first (see Next Steps).
- We don't know how long ago people joined. Anyone who joined many months ago may need a short reminder of who we are.

## 3. Key Messages

**Core message:**
> An AI assistant built for your business. We set it up, we look after it every month, and you stay in control.

**Supporting messages:**

1. **It's built around how you work.** We don't sell one ready-made tool. We start with a call, learn your daily tasks, and build an agent for them.
2. **We look after it for you.** The monthly fee covers checking how it's working, fixing problems, and updating it as your business changes. You don't need to learn the tech.
3. **It handles the repetitive tasks.** Things like answering common questions, sorting enquiries, and sending follow-ups. That frees your time for the work only you can do.
4. **You stay in control.** You decide what the agent does and what it doesn't. Important things can come to you to check first. Like any tool, it can make mistakes, so we set it up with that in mind.

**The free setup call:** 30 minutes, with no cost and no pressure. We talk about your tasks and where an AI assistant could save you time. You leave with a clear plan, whether you work with us or not.

**Proof points:** We have no client results we can show yet, so we won't use numbers, reviews, or "results" in these emails. We'll use these instead:
- Clear, honest examples of tasks an agent can do, labelled as examples
- A plain step-by-step of how the service works
- A clear list of what the monthly fee includes
- An honest note about limits (it can make mistakes, and it needs your input at the start)

When we have real client results we can prove, we'll add them in later campaigns.

**Message by group (first lines of emails):**
- **Small business owners:** "If you spend your evenings answering the same customer questions, this is for you."
- **Online store owners:** "'Where's my order?' Imagine an assistant that answers that for you, while you check anything important."
- **Agencies:** "Think about the client updates and reports your team writes every week. An AI assistant can handle the first draft."

## 4. Channel Strategy

**Email (the only channel).** This fits well. These people asked to hear from us, the list is small enough to make emails personal, and email costs very little.

| Approach | Why | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| **Plain, personal emails** | They read like a note from Abdul Manan, not a big brand. They work better for a small warm list and are less likely to land in spam. | Low |
| **Three versions of key emails** | Each group gets examples from its own kind of business. | Medium |
| **"Just reply" as a second way in** | Some people won't book from a link but will reply with a question. Every reply is a chance to book a call. | Medium (replies take time) |
| **Personal follow-up to clickers** | People who clicked the booking link but didn't book get a short personal note. This is likely the biggest source of extra bookings. | Medium |

**What we're not using:** paid ads, social media, blog. If the email results are good, social posts and a blog post about common questions could come in a later campaign.

**Other KPIs:**

| Metric | Rough guide for a warm list | How we track it |
|---|---|---|
| Open rate | 40% or more | Email tool reports |
| Click rate to the booking page | 5% or more per email | Email tool reports |

These guides are rough targets, not facts about our list. After this campaign, we'll have our own numbers to use.

**When to check:** Every Monday morning, compare bookings to the weekly check-point. If we're behind, see the Risks section.

## 8. Budget

The budget is small, so the main cost is time, not money.

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Email tool | Many tools have a free or low-cost plan for a list of 500. Check your current tool first. |
| Booking tool | Free plans are usually enough for this. |
| Writing and reviewing 6 emails (plus versions) | Your time: about 1 to 2 days in the prep week |
| Replies and personal follow-ups | Your time: about 30 to 60 minutes a day in weeks 1 and 2 |
| Setup calls | Your time: up to 30 calls x 30 minutes = 15 hours |

**Reserve:** Keep a few hours free each week in weeks 1 and 2 for extra replies and calls. That's when most bookings should come in.

## 9. Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | What could happen | What we'll do |
|---|---|---|
| **Holidays** | Weeks 3 and 4 get very few opens or bookings | Front-load the work into weeks 1 and 2. Offer January call times. Or move the start to Monday 4 January 2027 (see Decisions). |
| **Waitlist has gone cold** | People forgot who we are, so open rates are low | Email 1 starts with a short reminder: "You joined our waitlist for AI assistants built for your business." |
| **Worries about AI** | People fear mistakes, cost, or losing the personal touch | Email 4 answers these honestly. "You stay in control" runs through every email. No big promises. |
| **Emails land in spam** | 500 emails sent at once from a quiet address | Use plain text, few links, and no hype words. Send Email 1 in two batches a few hours apart. |
| **Behind target after week 1** | Fewer than 10 bookings by 13 December | Do more personal follow-ups. Test a new subject line. Look at which group responds best and send more to them. |
| **People book but don't show up** | Calls are booked but missed | Send a reminder email the day before each call. |
