## Send rules

- **Anyone who books a call stops getting these emails right away.** This is the main exit.
- Send Email 1 in two batches, a few hours apart, to help it stay out of spam.
- Don't send to anyone who has unsubscribed.
- If someone replies with a question, answer them personally. Their reply is a better chance to book than the next email.

## Tests to try

Keep tests simple. With 500 people, small differences won't mean much, so only trust a clear winner.

1. **Subject line for Email 1.** Send batch one with "We're open, and you get first pick" and batch two with "Your first pick of call times is here". Compare open rates.
2. **Reply vs. link in Email 2.** Count how many people book from the link and how many reply. This shows which way in your list prefers.
3. **Email 4 length.** If replies show one worry comes up again and again, try a shorter Email 4 that answers just that one.

## What to track

Use the targets in `plan.md`. The main ones:

- **Calls booked** (main goal): 10 by 10 January, 18 by 17 January.
- **Open rate:** 40% or more.
- **Click rate to the booking page:** 5% or more per email.
- **Replies:** count them and note the main topics.
- **Agency replies:** count "for my team" vs. "for my clients".
- **Unsubscribes:** under 1% per email.

Check bookings every Monday morning against the weekly targets.
